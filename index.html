Our Story

Who would've thought... that one random night on Litmatch... would introduce me to someone living hundreds of kilometers away.

You... from Cagayan Valley.

Me... from Nueva Ecija.

Different places. Different lives. Yet somehow... the same conversation found us.

When we first talked... I never imagined you would become one of my favorite people to receive notifications from.

I met you during one of your hardest days.

I don't know if I ever said the right words.

But if my presence made your heart a little lighter... then I'm thankful.

Because sometimes people don't need someone to solve everything.

Sometimes... they only need someone who chooses to stay.

Coffee Memories

We both love coffee. Funny isn't it?

We haven't shared a coffee table yet.

We haven't argued about who pays.

We haven't laughed over one cup together.

But somehow... coffee always reminds me of you.

Maybe someday... we'll stop sending coffee pictures and finally drink one together.
